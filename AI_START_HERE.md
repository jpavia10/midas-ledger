# Midas Ledger: start here (any AI)

Money: cash, debt and the way out of it. The book is encrypted; no AI sees real balances outside it.

1. **Read the rules for every pillar first:** https://github.com/jpavia10/prometheus-hub/blob/main/AI_START_HERE.md (where everything is, how files are named, how they are checked).
2. **Your homework here:** `docs/agents/QUEUE_{your id}.md` in this repo, or your section of the Drive doc `Prometheus Hub/Pillars/Midas Ledger/README — Midas Ledger`. Your homework in every pillar on one page: Drive `Prometheus Hub/{your folder}/HOMEWORK — {your name} (every pillar)`.
3. **Save what you make** in Drive `Prometheus Hub/Pillars/Midas Ledger/incoming (AI drop files)/` as `midas_{ai}_{what}_{YYYYMMDD-HHMM}.{ext}`. Code: a branch `{ai}/{what}` and a pull request on `jpavia10/midas-ledger`.
4. **Look:** the Prometheus look (cool midnight, depth, light; no neon, no Titan gold).
5. **Private pillar.** No real balances, account numbers, message text or positions anywhere but the pillar's own encrypted or on-device store. Homework here uses made-up data or public research only.
